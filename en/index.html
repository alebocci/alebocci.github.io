01 · Profile

Designing software that knows where and how it should run

My research focuses on engineering distributed software systems that run across heterogeneous infrastructures. A common thread is turning differences in capacity, reliability, energy use, cost, and trust into actionable information for placement, decomposition, orchestration, and execution decisions.

I work with declarative models, service-oriented architectures, open-source prototypes, and reproducible experiments. In recent years, I have extended these topics from the Cloud–Edge continuum to quantum cloud infrastructures.

02 · Research

Heterogeneous infrastructures, observable requirements, reproducible software

Three interconnected research directions, united by a focus on non-functional requirements, reproducibility, and the development of practically reusable tools.

01

Quantum software engineering

Methods and infrastructures to distribute, optimise, and make the execution of quantum circuits reproducible across heterogeneous QPUs: circuit cutting, shot distribution, benchmarking, hardware-aware selection, and adaptive execution control.

  • Quantum cloud
  • QPU selection
  • Circuit cutting
  • Benchmarking
02

Cloud–Edge and sustainability

Placement and simulation of distributed applications across the Cloud–Edge continuum, with a particular focus on FaaS orchestrations, energy-data observability, resource consumption, and microservice architectures.

  • Cloud–Edge
  • FaaS
  • Observability
  • Green software
03

Software security

Security-by-design for distributed applications: placement informed by information flows and provider trust, threat modelling, secure partitioning, and trustworthy execution platforms.

  • Secure placement
  • Information flow
  • Threat modeling
  • Trusted execution

Quantum software

Cut&Shoot

Distributes quantum-circuit fragments and shots across multiple NISQ computers, reconstructing the final result and analysing trade-offs among parallelism, overhead, and quality.

Benchmarking

QSimBench

A library, dataset, and set of procedures for reproducibly comparing quantum software across algorithms, circuits, shot budgets, and different backends.

Energy data

ODA

A microservice architecture for acquiring, transforming, aggregating, and making heterogeneous energy data observable, developed within the NEST project.

Secure placement

FaaS2Fog

A declarative prototype for determining placements of FaaS chains while accounting for hardware, software, latency, data-sensitivity, and provider-trust requirements.

Simulation
